
====================  ENCRYPTED TOOL-CALLING DEMO  =====================

  Scenario: A medical AI agent assists with treatment decisions.
  The agent uses two privacy-preserving tool calls:
    1. PIR -- look up drug interactions (server can't see which drug)
    2. FHE -- compute patient risk score (server can't see features)
  All decisions are made locally by the agent.
